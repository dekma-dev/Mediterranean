Clear the remaining raiders from the northern camp. The captain must fall as well; the anchor cannot be secured while any of the three remain.

When the camp is clear, approach the northern crystal and press E. Stay grounded and still for three seconds. Moving, jumping, fighting or taking a hit breaks your concentration, but you can try again.

Return to this beacon after the crystal has stabilized. The ward armor, gold and experience shown below are reserved for that completed work.
