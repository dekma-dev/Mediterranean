The northern crystal holds the astral anchor that steadies this frontier. Its light has begun to falter, and the raider camp stands between us and the work that must be done.

Defeat all three raiders, including their captain. Victories you have already earned count toward this task. Then follow the road to the northern crystal and press E beside it. Stand on the ground and remain still for three seconds while the anchor settles.

Movement, a jump, an attack or incoming damage will interrupt the work. If that happens, regain your footing and begin again. Once the crystal is stable, return here to collect your reward.

You will receive gold, experience and Astral ward armor. The armor will be equipped when you claim it, and your wounds will be tended. A secure outpost will also reopen the eastern sparring post for practice.
