Beyond these walls, the frontier road belongs to whoever is willing to defend it. Our supply parties have seen raiders gathering north of Dawnwatch. If we leave them unchallenged, the next caravan may never reach the beacon.

Follow the road out of the village and defeat one raider. Watch your enemy's stance: the orange warning marks a committed attack. Step clear or interrupt it with a heavy strike. Search the fallen for supplies, then return to me with your report.

You will receive gold, experience and a Watchguard blade from the outpost stores. The blade goes into your bag; equip it when you are ready. Your work will also open the way to a more urgent task at the northern crystal.
