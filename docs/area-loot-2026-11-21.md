# Nearby spoils and accepted-reward feedback

## Player-facing change

One E interaction now collects every eligible corpse within the original
2.5 m reach, instead of only the nearest corpse. A brass-framed HUD card shows
the number of accessible remains and their existing gold/potion contents.
Its collect button is a real command, and can explicitly collect nearby loot
when E belongs to a neighbouring NPC or quest object. NPC and quest E priority
is preserved. The card never captures movement or stops the world.

After collection, the card displays the amounts actually received, not a
before/after inventory estimate. A four-second receipt fades over its last
0.6 seconds. A partly full bag shows the accepted amounts and the number of
unclaimed bundles, with a retry button. If nothing fits, the header explicitly
says that the bag is full. Opening major panels hides the card; expiry removes
its pointer and nameplate reservations. All added strings have Russian entries.

## Transaction rules

- Eligible remains must be dead, unlooted, in reach and in line of sight.
  Training targets and rehearsal/sparring sessions cannot award these rewards.
- Nearest-first processing uses entity ID to break ties. Each existing raider
  bundle remains atomic: one potion must fit before its five gold or looted
  flag changes. A rejected bundle stays on the corpse for a later retry.
- A full bag does not prevent collecting the existing two-gold wildlife
  reward. The persistent finite-wildlife reward mask still prevents farming
  the same reward after respawn or load.
- The receipt is transient; loading does not replay it. The original corpse
  flags, inventory and economy accounting remain the save authority. No save
  version, reward value, loot radius or drop table was changed.
- Preview and collection share one eligibility predicate. The button
  re-evaluates the world at execution, so stale UI cannot grant remote loot.

## References and scope

[Blizzard's official looting explanation](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/4736886/dev-watercooler-mists-of-pandaria-looting-explained)
describes collecting nearby eligible corpses together after a group fight.
That interaction principle informed this change; it does not import personal
loot, raid-roll rules or Blizzard's reward tables. Our range remains 2.5 m.
The locally extracted `build-learning-review/pve2/frames/f000004.jpg` was
reviewed for compact framed HUD elements around an unobstructed central fight.
That sampled frame does not show a loot window and is not evidence of identical
loot behaviour. The card reuses our existing frame skin and potion icon;
models, faces, icons and class kits were not edited or downloaded.

## Verification

Final validation on 2026-11-21:

- InterfaceTests, SimulationTests, AreaLootTests, InventoryTests and
  PopulationTests passed in Release (35.49 seconds) and Visual Studio Debug
  (118.74 seconds).
- After the final truthful empty/full receipt captions and real-hit regression,
  AreaLootTests passed again in Release (5.03 seconds) and Debug (17.63 seconds).
- Final game builds: `build-ninja-release/RPGNative.exe` and
  `build-local/Debug/RPGNative.exe`. Available/received/full 1280px captures,
  partial 960px and available ultrawide captures were visually inspected.
- `git diff --check` passed. Localization coverage still reports the ten
  pre-existing engine diagnostic strings; the added loot text is translated.
- No commit, push, downloads or system changes were performed by the agent.

AreaLootTests exercises E/button collection, exact bundle accounting, repeated
requests, save/load, receipt expiry, partial/full bags, retry, wildlife rewards,
range/living exclusions and scenery obstruction. Native captures cover the
available, received, expired, partial and full-bag states at 1280x720, 960x600 and
2560x1080 in `area-loot-review` under the build output. Native pointer checks
verify the collect button and release of the expired card's input region.
It also uses actual attack contacts to defeat three low-health, stunned
raiders, then collects and reloads their rewards. This isolates the complete
hit/death/loot/save chain and does not measure live encounter difficulty.
The obstruction check uses accessible ground on opposite sides of a real tree
trunk within looting range. A stale request after the corpses move out of reach
grants nothing. Beside the keeper, E retains dialogue priority and the explicit
collect command neither opens nor confirms a quest dialogue.

Manual acceptance: finish a live group fight, approach scattered bodies,
collect using E and the card, open the bag to free space, and verify the retry
while other enemies remain active. These checks complement the isolated
transaction fixtures; they do not establish encounter balance.
