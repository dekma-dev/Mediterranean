## Implementation

- Added four original analytic ground materials: fractured frost with traveling cold veins, cooling ember fissures, interlaced holy tracery with opposed light chases, and an arcane spiral. Their shape is evaluated in the shader rather than sampled from a rotating image. Birth expands from the accepted contact centre; opacity and motion follow the effect's existing lifetime.
- A 4×4 terrain patch samples the local world height. The original spell boundary remains separate and accurate: the active frost field still has a three-metre radius and its simulation-owned five-second timer. Ten growing spatial crystals and low drifting wisps give it height. Its animation now follows field age rather than the unrelated global scene clock.
- Short-lived contact aftermath shares the same materials, with school-specific ascending light, cooling sparks and thawing facets. It retains the existing 180-vertex per-patch and 900-vertex crowd allowance, renders after important warnings/contacts, and creates no new damage area.
- Twelve field records occupy 384 bytes of constant-buffer data. Each terrain patch costs 96 vertices and joins the existing effects/glare batches. No new texture, offscreen target, per-field draw call, asset download or model edit is involved. The shared 6,144 effect-vertex ceiling is unchanged. Opaque depth also masks the field's glare emission.
- Native assertions cover internal motion, school distinction, deterministic repeats, invisible birth, invalid input, opaque occlusion, isolated portrait rejection and bounded submission. Existing real-cast, status lifetime, ground aftermath, warning-priority and frame-rate checks remain in force.
