# Native smoke test

Status: interactive checklist NOT RUN. Debug/Release Ninja/MSVC builds, ten CTest suites, and 122 smoke captures per configuration (worlds, panels, outfits, sparring, shop, close-up poses and environment views) pass as of 2026-10-18. Those automated checks do not replace this checklist. Separate integration captures show item dragging and defeat recovery. MotionTests covers 30/60/144-Hz acceleration, pose settling, windup alignment, menu/focus stops and camera response.

Run `./scripts/build-native.ps1 -Backend Ninja -Configuration Debug -Test -Run` from PowerShell. Repeat for Release after the Debug checks succeed. Record actual observations in `progress.md`; source inspection does not count as a pass.

1. Confirm the application shows a lit island, buildings, a blue player, red raiders, and readable HUD text. Check shader/device diagnostics if startup fails.
2. Resize, maximize, minimize, and restore the window. Confirm the view keeps its aspect ratio, the HUD remains usable, and rendering resumes.
3. Move with WASD, sprint, and jump. Try running and dashing into buildings and island edges. Confirm no tunneling or falling through the ground.
4. Orbit and zoom the camera. Move it toward buildings and trees and confirm that it pulls forward before intersecting their collision bounds. Small decorative dock/arena props are non-solid.
5. Hold a movement key, switch focus away, release it, and return. Confirm movement does not remain stuck and enemies did not damage the player in the background.
6. Press E at the gold beacon to accept the patrol. Approach a raider and cycle targets with Tab. Verify selected-target feedback and range rejection without consuming an attack cooldown.
7. Use strike and heavy strike; verify damage, cooldowns, enemy retaliation, and death. Check that melee attacks do not pass through buildings. Use mend after taking damage; full-health mend should not consume cooldown.
8. Defeat a raider, collect its loot with E, return, and claim the patrol reward. Repeated E presses must not add more loot or quest rewards. Also test defeating a raider before accepting the quest. Press G to equip the reward sword; confirm the character panel and subsequent damage reflect it.
9. Open C/I (or B)/J/M panels. Close them with X or the same hotkey. Escape opens the menu from gameplay or any panel, and Escape again closes the menu. Verify enemies and cooldowns continue while every panel/menu is open. Menu/atlas/journal capture movement/combat commands; character/bag allow them. Check every menu button, Save/Load feedback and explicit Exit.
10. Use Q while injured and check potion consumption/cooldown. Save with F5, move or fight, then load with F9. Confirm position, health, enemy state, loot claims, quest, gold, potions, equipment, and cooldowns are restored. Confirm a malformed save is rejected without replacing active progress.
11. Die and check that movement and abilities are disabled. Press Enter: return to the village with earned maximum health, quests, XP, equipment, gold, potions, defeated enemies, and loot claims retained. Living enemies should recover at home. Separately use R and confirm the entire current expedition resets without erasing the old save file.
12. Close the window and confirm the process exits. Review Debug output for DirectX warnings or errors.
13. Pull a raider around a building and beyond its home leash. Check navigation, separation between enemies, return behavior, and health restoration at home.
14. Watch a raider windup, stand in its cone for one hit, then dodge a later swing by moving or dashing outside it. Heavy strike should interrupt an active windup. Check floating numbers, hit flash, and the target's windup message. Returning enemies should be untargetable and immune.
15. Claim the patrol reward and check level 2, increased maximum health, restored health, and damage bonuses. Accept the next quest with E. Clear the remaining enemies, including the violet captain with a longer windup and heavier damage.
16. Start anchor stabilization with E at the violet crystal after all three enemies die. Move or use an ability to cancel; confirm no credit. Start again and stand still for three seconds. The crystal should turn green and the tracker should direct you back to the beacon.
17. Hand in the anchor quest. Check 50 additional gold, 120 additional XP, level 3, 140 maximum health, and ward armor in the character/inventory panels and on the model. Repeated E must not repeat the reward. Save/load the complete campaign and verify both quests and equipment persist.
18. Inspect the tracker, health/XP bars, contextual E prompt, target panel, journal, map, and death overlay at 960x600 and a larger window. Check that long messages fit and input remains usable with a panel open.

19. Press F2, orbit and zoom to inspect character silhouettes, armor seams, roofs, foliage, island boundaries and shadows. Press F2 again. Check that the menu, panels and death remain readable even with the HUD hidden. Watch F3 GPU time/draw calls in Release while moving and fighting; record resolution and hardware with any performance observation.

Known prototype limits: procedural stylized art and fixed-length joint poses; no audio, skeletal skinning or planted-foot animation; static-grid navigation and soft separation; decorative props are non-solid (the shop counter is solid); a small item catalog and six worn slots, without permanent disposal; level cap 3; no campaign respawns or networking. A separate nonlethal training encounter is repeatable without rewards. Hidden stationary, moving-camera, combat and merchant measurements exist, but interactive performance is not yet established.

20. Verify a normal launch fills the monitor without window borders. Press F11 to return to the previous window size and position, then toggle again. Test the menu Display button, Alt-Tab, DPI scaling and another connected monitor if available. No desktop resolution or scaling preference should change.
21. In I, select owned items and inspect descriptions/stack counts. Switch between both swords with Equip; confirm stats, world model and C preview change. Use a draught when injured; verify exactly one is consumed, cooldown applies and full-health use is disabled. Hover slots and action-bar buttons for details. Check that clicks do not pass through an open panel to the HUD.
22. In M, compare the illustrated roads/buildings/crowns/ruin/dock/counter with the actual map. Place a waypoint on clear ground, try invalid ground without losing the first waypoint, then clear it with right click or the button. Inspect player-facing and living/dead/loot/objective/quartermaster markers. Verify C shows six actual worn slots and real attributes, including head/off-hand gear when equipped.

23. Press C then I: character stays on the left, bag opens on the right, and the world stays undimmed. Walk and use Dash with both open. Orbit/zoom over the world gap; moving the pointer onto either window must capture camera input. Close the bag with X and check the character remains. Reopen the bag, open/close Esc, and check both windows return. Click worn gear to inspect it in the bag. Verify HUD tooltips do not appear through covered window surfaces.

24. Drag a stowed sword to the last bag cell, then to Weapon. The previous sword must return to the source cell. Drag worn gear into an empty bag cell and verify the source equipment slot becomes empty. Drop a potion onto Weapon and boots onto Chest: nothing should change. Use G during a sword drag; the stale drop must not move a different item. Drop outside all windows, press Esc mid-drag, Alt-Tab mid-drag and resize/toggle F11 mid-drag; none should cause a later accidental drop.
25. Right-click ward armor/tunic to swap, remove boots and trousers, and inspect both character views and attributes. Removing the sword must use unarmed damage; neutral underclothes remain when clothes are removed. Sort the bag and check item/potion totals. Save, rearrange/remove several items, reload, and verify the exact cells and worn slots restore. No equipped item should also appear in the bag.
26. Complete both quests, visit the cyan eastern training marker and press E. Verify the guard is targeted and the 30-second timer appears. Compare a normal hit with ward armor (6) and without it (8). Use heavy strike to interrupt and move/dash out of the cone. Check camera orbit beneath the nearby tree and near the house; it must not collapse into the player while retreating under foliage.
27. Defeat the guard, stop with E, leave the arena, let the timer expire and yield at 1 HP in separate rounds. Verify each report and unchanged XP/gold/loot/campaign kill count. Restarting must not heal. Potion use must consume a potion and retain cooldown. Save during a round, then load: practice ends while health/resources/gear remain as saved. Confirm nearby unclaimed campaign loot can still be collected with E.

28. Open/close Esc while right-clicking a bag item or clicking where the menu Exit button was. No hidden item or Exit action should fire. Repeat around C/I hotkeys and loading. Start RMB on the bag or minimap and drag into the world: the camera must stay still until RMB is released and pressed again over the world. Alt-Tab or toggle F11 while orbiting; return with RMB held and confirm the cancelled gesture does not resume. Before earning the reward sword, remove the starter blade and press G; it must equip again.

29. Visit the orange quartermaster marker near `(3, -12)` and press E while grounded. Gear should require the patrol hand-in. With the patrol complete, buy a helm for 20 and shield for 35. Check the corresponding charge, one bag item per purchase, depleted stock and disabled Buy. Open C alongside the shop and bag; equip both by drag/right-click and verify the world, portrait and armor attribute. Rotate the portrait both ways, then reset Front.
30. With ward, helm and shield worn, verify ordinary sparring damage is 3; removing all three gives 8. Save/load and respawn must preserve spent gold, stock and equipment. Buy draughts and drink one while injured; loading the subsequent save must not refund it or restock. Insufficient gold and sold-out clicks must change nothing. R creates fresh stock and removes purchased gear with the rest of the expedition.
31. While shopping, try movement, attacks and clicking the underlying action bar; they must be captured while world time continues. C/I must independently show/hide the side windows without closing trade. E or X closes only trade; walking must work again. Esc opens the menu and closes trade. Walk/dash into the physical counter and orbit around it; actors and camera must respect it. Check all three windows at 960x600 and fullscreen for clipped labels and obstructed controls.

32. Drag a stowed sword over Weapon, press F11 and release the mouse together. The sword must stay in its original location. After the display change, a fresh drag must equip normally. Repeat with a bag-to-bag move.
33. At low health, open the menu, atlas and journal in separate fights and let an enemy finish its attack. Defeat must close the central panel, cancel any drag/orbit and reveal the recovery prompt. Reopen each panel while defeated and press Enter: return to Dawnwatch with progress retained. Opening the menu after death must still allow Save/Load/Exit before choosing recovery.
34. Select an odd potion stack (for example five), click Split half, and check counts of three and two in separate cells. Select the second cell and drink while injured; it becomes one and the first stays three. Save/load must retain both exact cells. Drag them together or Sort to merge. A single potion cannot split. Full health or cooldown must consume neither stack.
35. At the quartermaster, select a stowed shield and Sell one for 17 gold. Its icon must move into Buyback and its original offer remain sold out. Hover for the name/price; click to reclaim for 17, returning it to the bag without equipping it. Repeating cannot create gold or extra gear. Worn items must show Unequip to sell; right-click still equips/removes/drinks.
36. Split potions, select the second stack and sell one. Only that cell should shrink; buyback shows one draught for 2 gold. Sell additional potions and verify the shelf count. Leave, reopen, save/load, and recover from death: holdings and gold must persist. Spend enough gold to make a shield buyback unaffordable; clicking it must retain the item and gold. Sell another item to fund reclaiming. A new expedition with R clears buyback.
37. Unequip and sell the reward blade and ward armor. XP/level and completed quests must remain unchanged, while damage/protection reflect actual worn gear. Save/load and reclaim both, then equip them again. If both swords are sold, G cannot equip either. Check the three-window buyback layout, hover text, Split half and Sell controls at 960x600, fullscreen and ultrawide.
38. With F2 hiding the HUD, orbit close to the character from the front, side and rear. Inspect the face/hair, collar, tunic trim, boots and helm fit. Walk, sprint, jump and strike with/without a shield: arms and legs must retain their lengths, feet must stay above the floor, and the sword grip/guard/blade must follow the hand. Check enemy windup/recovery and the captain's helm. Open C, rotate the preview and remove/re-equip clothes and armor; check both views and small-window clipping. Record sliding, abrupt transitions or intersections for animation refinement; the current solver does not provide foot planting or authored clips.
39. Run `.\scripts\build-native.ps1 -Backend Ninja -Configuration Release -BenchmarkSuite` and inspect `build-ninja-release/Release-benchmark-suite.txt`. Confirm walking distance and combat windup/hit counts are nonzero, then compare the four scene costs and resource bytes with docs/graphics.md. Separately play fullscreen in Release with F3 while moving/fighting and using the three-window merchant. Record resolution, hardware and visible stutters; the hidden suite excludes setup/load times and does not establish input latency or long-session stability.
40. Let a raider approach and stop within melee range. Its feet must stop walking during recovery and windup even when it retains a navigation route. Run beyond its leash and watch it return: it must turn toward the path home and stop walking on arrival. Repeat the stationary check with the sparring guard.
41. At low health, use a normal strike just before a raider's attack lands and defeats you. The damage from both attacks must apply, the recovery prompt must remain reachable, and your character must not keep the sword frozen in the raised strike pose. Press Enter and verify campaign progress is retained.
42. Follow the paving from the plaza through the village, then the dirt routes to the dock, eastern training post and northern anchor. Compare each junction with M. Roads must avoid buildings, vegetation must leave the services and sparring area readable, and the dock boards must clear the nearby trunk and house. Load an existing save and verify the same routes/cover remain in place without changing inventory or quests.
43. With F2/F3, inspect leaves from above and below while orbiting beneath trees and around shrubs. Look for missing back faces, grass disappearing near the camera, flickering shadows or paving shimmer during movement. Grass intentionally does not cast tiny shadows. Compare Release moving-camera costs with the recorded environment benchmark; captures alone cannot establish temporal stability or final art quality.
44. Walk around all four buildings with F2 hiding the HUD. Check shutters, side windows, timber/gable trim, the watch-hall crest, braced storehouse doors and training emblem from both sides. Compare roof colors with M. Lanterns are emissive props without local light sources. Doors and the filled ruin gateway must remain visibly closed, consistent with collision.
45. At the supply stall, orbit below and above the cloth and past each upright. The camera must avoid posts/counter/canopy while passing through the open bay and clear sky. The ordinary camera from the merchant interaction position must retain its distance. Open and close E/C/I, buy/equip gear and inspect the three-window layout at small/fullscreen sizes. Repeat after loading an existing save.
46. Start a fresh expedition and speak to the dressed keeper beneath the gold marker. Complete both quests and return; his presentation must not change interaction range, rewards or save compatibility. The keeper is a static NPC presentation, without dialogue animation or AI behavior yet.
47. Tap and hold WASD, switch to/from Shift and reverse direction. Motion should begin immediately with a short speed ramp, turns should follow the short arc and feet should settle on release. Position must stop immediately, including opening Esc while running. Push into a house, then leave the wall: no stored-speed burst. Repeat diagonally, airborne and after Alt-Tab. Dash should not produce several rapid walking steps.
48. Pull a raider around corners, retreat beyond its leash and watch it turn/return. Check for path-point pauses, sideways skating, home-arrival pops and sudden separation jumps. During a windup its body and orange cone must share a fixed direction; move behind it, dash away and interrupt another swing. Repeat with the captain and training guard.
49. Orbit across the rear angle and zoom both ways; look for smooth short transitions without overshoot. Jump and land beside a building/tree/stall. The camera must retract immediately before clipping geometry and ease back out after clearing it. Check narrow gaps and rapid alternating wheel input. Load mid-windup, respawn and restart: no old walking/attack blend or camera flight from the previous state. These are physical playtest items; static PNGs cannot validate their feel.
50. Hold W while rapidly orbiting with RMB. Movement must remain forward relative to the displayed view. Open Esc/J/M mid-orbit, then close it while still holding RMB: no trailing rotation or resumed old drag. Repeat through Alt-Tab and F11; a fresh RMB press must restore normal orbit.
51. Slowly sweep the camera across a tree/post edge, briefly exposing and hiding its path. It should retract immediately and stay steady through brief clearance, then recover after about 100 ms of continuous clearance. Scroll inward during that hold; zoom must still respond. Jump, land, walk and sprint: compare consistent jump height and settled body/feet without a sharp bob at neutral.
52. Pull the western raider around the tree at (-14, 10), then stand close on the opposite side of its trunk. It must walk around to see/reach you rather than stop permanently at melee distance. In open ground, strafe while an enemy follows: its route must track you continuously, without periodic corrections to old positions. Let it begin a swing; the cone must still stop turning until impact.
53. In Release, hide the HUD with F2 and inspect feet, small stones, doorstep edges and dock boards. Contact shadows should remain close to the objects and soften at their edges. Walk, jump, turn and attack beside a tree or building, then watch a raider do the same. Shadows must follow the current pose without trails or material-dependent disappearance. Very small detail is limited by the 1024-square map; record temporal shimmer separately from static edge quality.
54. Orbit close to village wall braces, window frames, roofs and the supply canopy. Look for diagonal self-shadow stripes, scalloped trim edges, light leaking through opaque roofs and detached shadows. Turn until a tree/building leaves the camera view while its shadow remains visible: the shadow must stay. The sun projection is fixed in world space; camera orbit/zoom must not slide the whole shadow pattern.
55. Open C and I in a shaded area, rotate the equipped preview, close the panels, then use F11 and Alt-Tab. World shadows must remain intact outside the portrait, preview lighting must remain independent, and resizing/refocusing must not retain a previous frame's shadow. Combat warnings and hovering quest effects should not cast dark solid shapes. Check with F3 during visible play; the hidden benchmark does not guarantee interactive FPS.
